01 / The case

One place to explore travel stories

Channel One is a major national media broadcaster with a digital video platform and subscription-based streaming service, comparable to RaiPlay in Italy.

The company had several TV shows dedicated to travelling across Russia, with a growing library of more than 1,000 videos. Based on user feedback and editorial needs, our team decided to create a dedicated platform where users could discover travel content from different programmes in one place.

The goal was to make the video library easier to explore and provide different ways to find information about cities, regions and places across Russia.

02 / The solution

Two ways to discover the content

I proposed two main ways for users to explore the video library: browse by region or navigate through an interactive map.

Journey 01

Search by region

Users could browse an alphabetical list of Russian regions, search for a location and open its dedicated page. Each page brought together videos from different travel shows related to that location.

Journey 02

Explore the interactive map

Users could select a geographical point on the map to open a related travel video. The video page also included more content from the same region, inviting users to continue exploring.

04 / Technical implementation

Connecting video content to places

The videos were already stored in the company's content management system, with standard metadata such as ID, title and description.

To support the new platform, developers introduced two additional geographical fields. These connected the video library to regional collections and map locations.

One challenge was inconsistent geographical information: some entries contained country names, others cities, regions or individual landmarks. I coordinated the work with the editorial team to review and standardise the geographical tags so that the regional pages and map displayed the correct content.

CMS metadata
PLACES

Identified the region associated with a video and supported regional video collections.

GEO_TAG

Stored geographical coordinates connecting a video to a point on the interactive map.

05 / Project management

Waterfall with iterative feedback cycles

The project followed a mainly sequential workflow, similar to the Waterfall approach. We moved through clearly defined stages: concept and requirements, content preparation, design, development, testing and launch. Each stage provided the basis for the next one.

However, content preparation and testing required a more iterative approach.

Content preparation

We worked closely with the editorial team, reviewed geographical tags, identified inconsistencies and corrected the data through several rounds of feedback.

Testing and launch

I tested the functionality, collected feedback, shared issues with developers and coordinated repeated rounds of corrections.

This allowed us to keep the overall project structured while making improvements whenever practical testing and feedback revealed a need for changes.

06 / The result

More than 1,000 travel videos, brought together in one place.

Users could explore the content through regional search, dedicated regional pages and an interactive map. The project transformed a fragmented video collection into a structured digital experience for discovering travel destinations, landmarks and routes across Russia.

The platform remains active and continues to expand its video library.
